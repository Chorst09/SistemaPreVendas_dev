# Product Overview

## Sistema de Prevendas (Pre-Sales System)

A comprehensive pre-sales management platform designed for IT service companies to create, analyze, and manage commercial proposals and pricing models.

### Core Purpose
Enable sales teams to quickly generate accurate proposals, perform financial analysis, and manage complex pricing scenarios for IT services including:
- Service Desk dimensioning and staffing
- NOC (Network Operations Center) pricing
- IT infrastructure assessments
- Commercial proposal generation
- Edital (RFP) analysis and response

### Key Features
- **Service Desk Calculator**: Erlang C-based dimensioning for support teams
- **Proposal Management**: Create, edit, and track commercial proposals
- **Financial Forecasting**: Multi-scenario budget and revenue projections
- **Edital Analysis**: AI-powered analysis of RFP documents
- **Team Scheduling**: Automated schedule generation and team management
- **PDF Generation**: Professional proposal export with branding
- **Dashboard**: Real-time metrics and sales tracking

### Target Users
- Sales managers and representatives
- Pre-sales engineers
- Finance and pricing teams
- Project managers

### Business Model
B2B SaaS platform for IT service companies. Supports multiple clients with customizable pricing models and templates.
